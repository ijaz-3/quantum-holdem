START WITH TWO WAVES

Two faces. Two quantum branches.

In the match, each card is a superposition of two complete faces—not a fixed rank with a random suit. Here |0⟩ means A♥ and |1⟩ means K♠. Amplitude length squared gives the chance of that whole face, including its rank. A gate changes the waves; a measurement samples a readout and changes the state. Choose an effect, then apply it to watch the change.

Why tune? On a flop A♦ 7♣ 3♠, with a 2♣ alongside, the A♥ branch gives you a pair; K♠ gives high card. Changing branch odds changes your rank-made hand. The guide below shows precisely how each operation changes those odds or phases. The challenges use complete-face rank choices, show the same Z commitment and two-move limits as the match, and keep linked inference separate from a public showdown reveal.

Why can a linked card stay 50/50?

Before an informative readout, each half of a Bell pair is locally mixed. H, Z, or S on your half can change the pair’s amplitudes and correlations without changing either card’s individual 50/50 face odds. After a phase measurement, your own half may be a known phase state: H can then turn it into a definite face. The opponent’s unconditional odds are not controlled remotely.

What does Z commit?

A Z read locks one complete face—rank and suit together. This guide stops that card’s route after Z, just like the match. X and Y return phase-basis readouts, leave rank and suit unresolved, and permit another gate if you have a move left. Reset to start a fresh preparation.

What exactly is linked?

The public pair uses A♥ and K♠ in both decks. It starts as (|A♥, K♠⟩ + |K♠, A♥⟩)/√2. Measuring your Z face initially predicts Nova’s opposite rank and suit. One H breaks that opposite-only relation into four possible pairs; both H gates make the coherent faces match. Z/S phase gates change phase correlations without immediately changing face odds. Private results are not remotely chosen.

Are linked cards always entangled?

No. They start entangled, but a projective readout makes this Bell pair separable. Conditional predictions or classical correlations can remain useful after that. The game still calls them “linked” because they share a two-card register and public contract. Later local gates can change faces or predictions; they cannot recreate the lost entanglement.

Which measurement is the complete one?

Z, X, and Y are separate complete bases for one two-outcome qubit. Z reads the complete candidate face; X reads plus/minus; Y reads +i/minus-i. Neither X nor Y directly names a card face. Other basis directions exist, but these three are used here. Readouts in incompatible bases need not preserve the old state.
