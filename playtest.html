No name, email, account, or automatic upload. If you consent, this device records your actions and feedback. Local submissions are self-reported; they are not independently verified participants.

  1. Try one guided challenge.
  2. Predict two unfamiliar hands without coaching.
  3. Play several live hands. Decide when a quantum move is worth spending.
  4. Come back here and give an honest response.

Exports contain local timestamps, action choices, prediction answers, and your responses. Share only if you choose. Session counts are not evidence of enjoyment or measured learning gains.
